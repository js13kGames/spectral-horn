> *When theoretical optics, procedural rendering, crystal unicorns, and an unforgiving 13-kilobyte limit collide.*

In 1666, Sir Isaac Newton stuck a glass prism into a sunbeam and discovered that white light is secretly a chaotic party of rainbow colors combined.

Naturally, we asked the only logical follow-up question of modern science: **What if, instead of a prism, we used a unicorn horn?**

## Controls & Steering
- **Move Unicorn:** Click / touch and drag the unicorn's body.
- **Rotate Horn:** Drag the circular **Orbit Ring** surrounding the horn.
- **Micro-Nudge:** Click the **`↺` and `↻` Step Buttons** for precise 0.5° fine-tuning.
- **Goal:** Aim the requested wavelength (or composite color) into the spectral sensors. Hold the beam steady until the sensor charges to 100% and locks!
- **Mobile Friendly:** Full touch support with vertical touch offset so your fingers never obscure the action.

## 🔬 Optical Mechanics (Real Physics, No Gimmicks)
Every ray in the game is simulated analytically using genuine wave and geometric optics:
- **Snell's Law of Refraction:** Exact vector ray-surface boundary refractions.
- **Cauchy Dispersion:** Light splits because the refractive index varies with wavelength (400 nm violet bends sharper than 700 nm red).
- **Total Internal Reflection (TIR):** Exceed the critical angle to trap light inside the crystal horn—essential for periscope trickshots around dark obsidian shields!
- **Dove Prisms:** Flip the color spectrum without deflecting the beam direction.
- **Spherical Lenses (Crystal Orbs):** Focus divergent rays into pinpoints or expand them into wide spectral fans.
- **Additive Color Alchemy:** Need yellow? Hit the sensor with a pure 580 nm beam, or overlap red and green rays additively!
